Every pair we make can be taken apart again. The welt is stitched, not
glued, so a cobbler can cut the old sole away and sew a new one on — the
upper, the part that has learned the shape of your foot, stays.

## What we repair

- **Resoling** — leather or rubber, as the pair was made.
- *Heel blocks*, rebuilt from the top lift down.
- Stitching that has worn through at the flex point.
